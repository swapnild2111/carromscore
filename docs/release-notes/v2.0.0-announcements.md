## Email

For sending to club WhatsApp/email lists, coaches, and tournament
organisers you know. Formal-ish but warm.

**Subject:** Carromscore 2.0 is live — free live scoreboard for your next club night

Hi [Name],

Quick note — I've just released Carromscore 2.0, and I wanted to
share it with you first since [we've talked about club scoring
before / you organise tournaments / you run a club night].

**Carromscore** is a free live scoreboard for carrom that runs on any
phone or laptop. Casual anonymous scoring for pickup games, plus a
new organiser layer for club nights and tournaments.

**What's new in 2.0:**

- **Live broadcast + spectator URL** — toggle Live on match setup and
  friends/family watch the score update in real time on any phone.
  Same URL feeds OBS/Prism for YouTube or Facebook Live streaming.

- **Tournament tags** — tag matches with an event name. Every match
  sharing a tag groups together in the /live/ lobby. Players and
  spectators find their game in one place.

- **Full match history** — every finished match is archived with the
  full board-by-board scorecard. Look up any match by player,
  tournament, or date.

- **Organiser panel** — sign in with Google, and matches tagged to
  your tournament get a ✎ edit affordance. Fix a wrong score, correct
  a queen credit, delete a scrubbed test match — without touching a
  database.

- **Practice mode** — solo drill sessions with a per-set miss matrix.
  Also broadcasts to Live so a club running the same drill can see
  everyone's numbers on a projector.

**What hasn't changed:**

- Still free forever.
- No accounts required to score a match — casual users just open the URL.
- No ads, no tracking, no data sold. Open source (MIT) on GitHub.

**Try it at your next club night** — it takes 30 seconds to score a
match, and you'll have a live URL you can share with anyone who wants
to watch.

- 🌐 Web: https://swapnild2111.github.io/carromscore/
- 📱 Android APK: https://github.com/swapnild2111/carromscore/releases/latest
- 📖 How-to guide: https://swapnild2111.github.io/carromscore/help/

If you'd like to be an organiser for a specific tournament (so you can
fix scoring mistakes on your event's matches), reply to this email
with the Google account you'd like to use and I'll set it up.

Would love to hear how it works for your club. Bug reports and feature
ideas via reply or via the in-app Feedback link.

Best,
Swapnil

# Install on iPhone / iPad

Carromscore works on iPhone and iPad without an App Store download.
Safari's built-in "Add to Home Screen" gives you an app icon that
launches Carromscore full-screen, exactly like a native app.

## Steps

1. Open Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Go to **https://swapnild2111.github.io/carromscore/**.
3. Tap the **Share** button (square-with-arrow icon at the bottom of
   the screen).
4. Scroll down in the share sheet and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Optional: rename to just "Carromscore" if it defaults to something
   longer.
6. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.

The Carromscore icon appears on your home screen. Tap to launch —
you'll see the app in full-screen with no browser chrome.

## Why is there no App Store version?

Apple's App Store policies discourage apps that are "just a web view"
(Guideline 4.2) and charge $99/year for a developer account plus a
review cycle for each submission. For a free hobby carrom app, that
overhead isn't worth it. Add to Home Screen gives you the same "app on
my home screen" experience without any of that friction.

## What works

- Full-screen app-like launch.
- All the scoring features — matches, practice mode, share URL,
  broadcast overlay.
- Offline play (once you've loaded the app at least once).
- Landscape lock during a match.

## What's different from Android

- **No auto-update banner.** iOS Safari updates web content
  automatically without a special notification. When Carromscore
  releases new features, you get them the next time you launch.
- **No "back" button when full-screen.** Use the **✕ Close** button in
  the footer of a match to leave the scoring screen.
- **No wake lock on older iOS.** Screen stays awake reliably on iOS
  16.4+. Older versions might dim during long matches; tap the screen
  or swipe up briefly to prevent it.
